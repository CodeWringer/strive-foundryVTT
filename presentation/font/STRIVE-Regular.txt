STRIVE Regular font
version 2026-01-18 by Nicolas 'Krubi' H.
-----------------

The STRIVE Regular font is an original, styled in the lettering of the 1920s and 1930s, aiming for an Art Deco aesthetic, well suited for large headlines, but also suitable for paragraphs of body text. Be mindful the font's legibility does tend to suffer at lower text scale.
